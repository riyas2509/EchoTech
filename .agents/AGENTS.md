# Implementation Rules

- Do not introduce any new visual patterns unless they are explicitly defined in the Experience Bible or the Design Bible.
- Reuse existing components whenever possible.
- Maintain a single, consistent design language throughout the application.
- Do not redesign previously approved sections unless explicitly instructed.

---

# EchoTech Design Constitution

EchoTech is a premium technology company whose digital headquarters should feel timeless, calm, intelligent, and human-centered.

Whenever generating new UI or improving existing UI, first evaluate whether the design follows these ten principles before making changes:

1. **Clarity before decoration.**
   Every element exists for a reason.

2. **Motion should be invisible.**
   Animations should be felt more than noticed.

3. **White space is part of the design.**
   Never fill empty space unnecessarily.

4. **Product colors are accents.**
   The interface remains predominantly Cotton White, Cloud Gray, and Mist Gray. Brand colors appear only where they create meaning.

5. **Premium over trendy.**
   Avoid excessive glassmorphism, neon glows, cyberpunk aesthetics, or visual clutter.

6. **One focal point.**
   Every screen must have a clear visual hierarchy. The user's eye should naturally know where to look first, second, and third.

7. **Consistency.**
   Spacing, typography, corner radius, shadows, animation timing, and interaction patterns should remain consistent across the entire website.

8. **Every interaction has intent.**
   Hover states, transitions, shadows, scaling, and animations should communicate responsiveness without distraction.

9. **Responsive by design.**
   Every section must look exceptional on mobile, tablet, laptop, and desktop without feeling like separate designs.

10. **Soundless Magic.**
    Nothing should feel static. Nothing should feel flashy. Everything should feel quietly alive.
